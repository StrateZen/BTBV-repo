# Product Vision

Room Energy Organizer is a client-facing transformation app for an organizing and energy-work business.

The core value is not task tracking alone. The product connects physical space, emotional impact, energy alignment, practical action, progress, and measurable value.

## Product Equation

`space -> emotion -> energy -> action -> progress -> measurable value`

## Primary Client Loop

1. Add a room.
2. Upload before photos.
3. Describe how the room feels.
4. Submit stress, clutter, and energy alignment scores.
5. Receive AI-assisted recommendations after Emily review.
6. Complete organizing and energy reset actions.
7. Upload after photos.
8. Submit updated emotional scores.
9. Watch room and whole-home KPIs improve.
10. Move to the next room or receive follow-up support.

## Roles

- Client: submits rooms, photos, emotions, scores, after updates, and tracks value.
- Emily: reviews AI recommendations, personalizes guidance, sends recommendations, prioritizes support, and flags upsells.
- AI Assistant: drafts observations, organizing recommendations, energy-work suggestions, next steps, follow-ups, and upsell opportunities.
- GoHighLevel: manages CRM contact state, membership status, tags, workflows, pipeline stages, nurture, booking, and upsell automation.
